Sunny Reddy at Wayne State

Sunny Reddy is an elected Governor of Wayne State University and a proud alumnus. He earned his Master's Degree in Chemical Engineering from Wayne State University in 1994.

His leadership reflects a strong commitment to academic excellence, student success, and maintaining the integrity and strength of higher education.

Publicly Stated Principles

Sunny champions STEM education as a pathway to opportunity, supporting programs that connect students with real-world skills and career readiness in technology, engineering, and innovation-driven fields.

Sunny believes higher education should be accessible to all qualified students. He supports initiatives that reduce financial barriers and ensure Wayne State remains an affordable, high-quality institution for Michigan residents.
