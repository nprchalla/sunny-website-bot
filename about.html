About Sunny

A Michigan-based entrepreneur and public leader serving on the Wayne State University Board of Governors, focused on practical leadership and long-term impact.

Quick Facts

  • J.D. (Law degree), Wayne State University School of Governors
  • B.A. in Political Science, Wayne State
  • Board Member, Michigan Opera Theatre
  • Education: Wayne State University (degree); MIT
  • Passionate about practical leadership

Public Service

As a Wayne State Board of Governors member, Sunny has worked to make higher education more accessible and affordable, championing initiatives that give students real-world pathways through education and practical experience.

Entrepreneurship

From practice management platforms to patient-focused tools, Sunny has built companies that directly meet community needs, driven by real-world challenges and tested by user feedback.

Community-Lifting Work

Sunny's approach centers on direct community engagement, supporting projects in Detroit and beyond that bring real value and last beyond short-term attention cycles.

The Person Behind The Work

It's in public places and windowless rooms where Sunny works, not glitzy galas or carefully curated press—the practical realities of what it takes to make change happen, the meetings, the follow-through, and the commitment to showing up. When he's not in a board meeting, you'll find him turning his sharp mind to whatever problem's in front of him.

While, like others, he's spent time navigating or thinking about complex policy decisions—things like access to healthcare, worker protections, economic development, or education. Sunny's drawn to work that reflects real outcomes, not empty promises.
