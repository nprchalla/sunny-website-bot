Who is Sunny Reddy?

Sunny Reddy’s journey is built on opportunity, hard work, and a deep belief in giving back. Originally from India, he came to the United States to pursue higher education and earned his Master’s degree in Chemical Engineering from Wayne State University. His experience as a student shaped his lifelong commitment to education and its power to open doors and transform lives.

A successful entrepreneur and business leader, Sunny founded and leads VOIP OFFICE, where he has helped drive innovation, create jobs, and support local businesses. Beyond business, he has remained actively engaged in philanthropy, supporting initiatives such as COVID-19 relief, disaster response efforts, and assistance for first responders and their families. For Sunny, leadership means using success to strengthen the community around him.

In 2024, Sunny was elected to the Wayne State University Board of Governors, beginning his service in 2025, where he focuses on student success, affordable education, campus safety, and strengthening STEM programs. He also serves as Co-Chair of the Michigan Republican Party. Above all, Sunny is a devoted family man, married to his wife, Sirisha, for over 30 years and proud father of a Yale graduate, whose personal journey continues to inspire his commitment to education, service, and opportunity for all.
