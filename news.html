The Person Behind The Work

It's in public places and windowless rooms where Sunny works, not glitzy galas or carefully curated press — the practical realities of what it takes to make change happen, the meetings, the follow-through, and the commitment to showing up. When he's not in a board meeting, you'll find him turning his sharp mind to whatever problem is in front of him.

While, like others, he's spent time navigating or thinking about complex policy decisions — things like access to healthcare, worker protections, economic development, or education — Sunny's drawn to work that reflects real outcomes, not empty promises.
